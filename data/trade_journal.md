# Trade Journal

## Purpose
Record winning and losing trades to identify patterns and improve future decisions.

## How to Use
After each closed trade, analyze what happened and record:
- Entry and exit conditions
- What signals worked
- What news/market conditions influenced the trade
- What you would do differently

- P&L: +X.X%

- [List the indicators/signals that predicted this move]

#### Market Context

- [Any relevant news?]
